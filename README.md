## What the lab is

ONDE AI R&D is an applied research and development laboratory. It studies how to work durably with an AI: work that stays under human authority and whose quality can be measured. "Work" covers any human–AI workflow (creating, deciding, organising, learning), not only producing deliverables.

It produces knowledge put to the test, always in pairs: a named mechanism, which explains; a test written in advance, which can contradict it. Its method: lived problems, refutable hypotheses, a gated pipeline. One practitioner-researcher, N=1, declared. Everything is recorded; not everything is published.

## The problem

AI-assisted work rarely fails for lack of model power. It fails on three points.

**Durability.** What works in one session often breaks by the tenth, and again at the next model generation; workflows get rebuilt. The lab's question: what repeats, and what survives a change of generation?

**Authority.** A project's canon drifts without a decision; an unvalidated proposal becomes the reference by repetition; memory swells, and the human ends up abdicating in fact. The lab's question: how is a reference earned by human decision, and how does it stay one?

**Measurement.** The quality of human–AI work is narrated more than measured: few indicators, few failure conditions. The lab's question: which indicator, and which failure conditions written before the measurement? The candidate indicator: corrective iterations per validated deliverable.

These three problems are observable and reproducible. The lab approaches them as mechanisms: nameable, testable, refutable.

## The stance

> "The AI proposes, the human decides: informed, guided, put in a position to decide well. Each decision moves the work forward and leaves its trace. Responsibility is the price; the safety of the decision is the gain. The workflow advances and repeats under human authority: personal or professional, same rule."

## The method, in one line

```
reflection → hypothesis → test bench → researcher field → auditable → real users → replicated → doctrine
```

Rank is earned by use, never self-assigned.
The gated pipeline is not the lab's invention; what is its own is the content of each stage and the criteria of each passage.
The apparatus that holds this method is called ACTA: an intake, the single entry where every problem, the founder's included, receives a human verdict; a journal; a register; protocols.
It is described in [`methode/PIPELINE.md`](methode/PIPELINE.md) (FR).

**[LIVING REFERENCE](https://github.com/JP-Noto/LIVING-REFERENCE)**, public: the status of knowledge in an AI-assisted project, from proposal to reference by validation.
**Doctrine**: in use since July 2026, replicated by its declared users, each on their own side on a system distinct from the author's, and left unchanged through a change of model generation.
A re-test can be requested if use shows the need; it is not systematic.
Taken up as an architecture piece by a third-party OS; every decision there refers to it and leaves its trace.

The four other corpora develop in private:

- **MYSTANCE**, the human layer: tuning the human–AI relation rather than dominating it; the assistance level modulates how much the AI proposes, never who decides.
- **WORKING REFERENCE**, the execution layer: a corpus is not a document one reads, it is a function one calls; a constant earned by gates, served and sealed.
- **SOUNDNESS**, the birth of knowledge extracted from documents: the record states nothing the source piece does not vouch for.
- **VIGILANCE**, the economy of attention: the system verifies everything, the human decides on exceptions only.

They open by invitation, within a collaboration, under a confidentiality agreement (NDA). Contact via the [profile](https://github.com/JP-Noto).

## The sequence

It has no date. Researcher field, then auditable (the corpus becomes public), then real users, then replication by third parties deploying from the corpus alone. A piece comes out when it is finished, with its protocol and its limits.

## The lab's guards

- **Declared use earns the rank, measurement says how much**: a mechanism is demonstrated in use when its declared users have adopted it; like an engine, once started, it runs. The effect on the indicator is a separate claim, measured afterwards.
- **Recency is not quality**: resemblance to the latest work is not a criterion of passage.
- **Ratings = signal**: evaluations trigger an examination; they are never the metric.
- **Everything recorded, not everything published**: what is published serves a demonstration or a reflection, with no timing obligation.
